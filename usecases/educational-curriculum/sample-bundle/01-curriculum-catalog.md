# Health Documentation Management — Module Catalog

This catalog node is the **normative layer** of the bundle. It holds the official learning outcomes (LO) and assessment criteria (AC) adapted in English from [Order EDU/10/2020](https://portaldogc.gencat.cat/utilsEADOP/PDF/8057/1780410.pdf) (DOGC no. 8057), preserving the official criteria structure.[^order-edu-10-2020] Agents MUST treat it as read-only truth: never invent or silently rewrite statutory criteria.

## Learning outcomes

### LO1 · Manage clinical records

Interpret and organize clinical records in a health institution.

#### Assessment criteria

<a id="ac11"></a>

**AC1.1 — Classify a clinical record by document type.**[^order-edu-10-2020] The learner assigns the correct document category and archive code.

<a id="ac12"></a>

**AC1.2 — Validate the completeness of a record.** The learner checks that every required field of a clinical record is present and coherent.

<a id="ac13"></a>

**AC1.3 — Apply the legal retention period.** The learner determines how long a record must be kept according to current regulations.
