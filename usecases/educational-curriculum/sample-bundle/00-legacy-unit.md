# Legacy Unit — Paper-Based Filing (Retired)

Under the LOGSE framework, learners practiced physical filing of paper records.[^rd-543-1995] In Catalonia this title was deployed by Decret 180/1998 (DOGC no. 2693, 31 July 1998), expressly repealed when the current curriculum was enacted. This unit is retained for traceability and points to its successor: the digitized workflow in [Lab — Classify and Validate a Clinical Record](02-learning-activity.md). Keeping it `status: deprecated` with `superseded_by` lets a coverage audit skip retired units without losing the historical record.
