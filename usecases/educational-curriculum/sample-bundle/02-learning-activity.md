# Lab — Classify and Validate a Clinical Record

This activity was drafted by an agent and confirmed by a teacher. It covers the three assessment criteria of learning outcome LO1 in the [curriculum catalog](./01-curriculum-catalog.md):[^curriculum-catalog] classify a record by document type ([AC1.1](./01-curriculum-catalog.md#ac11)), validate its completeness ([AC1.2](./01-curriculum-catalog.md#ac12)), and apply the legal retention period ([AC1.3](./01-curriculum-catalog.md#ac13)).[^curriculum-catalog]

## Materials

- One anonymized clinical record per learner (paper or digital).
- The module catalog, open at LO1.
- A checklist that mirrors AC1.1–AC1.3.

## Procedure

1. **Classify** — each learner assigns a document category and an archive code to their record, following AC1.1.
2. **Validate** — the learner checks that a mandatory field is present and coherent in every record, following AC1.2.
3. **Retain** — the learner states the legal retention period for the record and where it should be archived, following AC1.3.

## Assessment

Use a simple observation checklist tied to the three criteria. Mark each learner as *achieved* or *not yet achieved* per criterion, and record the outcome in the course evaluation rubric. Because every criterion of LO1 is covered here, a coverage audit finds **no orphan criteria**.
